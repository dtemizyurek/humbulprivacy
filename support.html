How can we help?

Answers for the most common Humbul questions.

Apple Health

Humbul only reads the Apple Health categories you explicitly allow. You can review or revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Humbul.

Meal analysis

AI-assisted meal estimates can be inaccurate. Review calories and macros before saving. Submitted meal photos are processed to return an estimate and are not intentionally stored in your Humbul cloud profile after analysis.

Premium purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. Use Restore Purchases in Humbul after signing in with the Apple account associated with your Humbul profile. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Account and data deletion

Open Profile → Account & Data → Delete Account. Humbul will ask you to authenticate again before deleting your account and cloud data. Deleting your account does not cancel an active App Store subscription.

Contact

For assistance, open an issue in the project’s public support tracker. Do not include health information, Apple receipts, passwords, authentication tokens or other sensitive personal data.
